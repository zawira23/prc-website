FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:

Q: What should I do with the completed checklist?
A: Keep it on file as a legal record. If a dispute arises over deposit deductions
   or damage claims, the inspection checklist provides proof of condition.

Q: Can I share the inspection report with tenants?
A: Yes. Print the report and give a copy to the tenant. Both should sign and date it.
   This protects both parties.

Q: How long should I keep inspections on file?
A: At least 2–3 years after a tenant moves out, in case disputes arise.

Q: What if I notice asbestos?
A: Do NOT disturb it. Note it in the report and advise the landlord to contact
   a specialist for testing and removal.

Q: Can I use the PDF on my phone?
A: Yes, open it in any PDF reader app. The interactive HTML app works better on
   larger screens (tablet or desktop).
