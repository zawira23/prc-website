Title:
The Legal Eviction Process in Ghana — A Plain-Language Guide for Landlords

Short Description:
Know the law. Follow the process. Recover your property. A complete step-by-step
guide to legal eviction in Ghana — from the first warning to the bailiff.

Full Description:
Most landlords in Ghana do not know the legal eviction process. Frustrated by
unpaid rent, they change locks, cut utilities, remove roofing, or give threats.
All of these are illegal under the Rent Act 1963 (Act 220) and expose landlords
to criminal prosecution.

This guide explains every step of the legal eviction process in plain English —
written for landlords with no legal background.

✔ Chapter 1: What the Law Says — Act 220, Rent Control, and why self-help
