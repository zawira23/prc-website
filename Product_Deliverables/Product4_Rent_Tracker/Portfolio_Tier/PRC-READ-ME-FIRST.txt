GET STARTED:

1. Open the Excel file: PRC-Rent-Tracker-Portfolio.xlsx

2. Go to the SETUP sheet and fill in:
   - Your grace period (default: 7 days)
   - Late fee type (Fixed Amount or Percentage)
   - Late fee amount
   - Maximum cap (optional)

3. Add your tenants in the TENANT REGISTER sheet

4. Each month, go to RENT ROLL and enter:
   - Period (YYYY-MM format)
   - Date Paid and Amount Paid for each tenant
   - Everything else calculates automatically

5. Check ARREARS REGISTER for unpaid accounts

6. Review DASHBOARD for monthly summary

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:

Q: Can I use this in Google Sheets?
A: Yes. Upload the .xlsx file to Google Drive. All formulas and dropdowns
   work identically.

Q: What if I make an error — can I edit a payment?
A: Yes. Simply update the Amount Paid or Date Paid cell. All calculations
   recalculate automatically.

Q: How do I archive old months?
A: Copy the rows from the Rent Roll and paste into a separate "Archive"
   sheet if desired. The tracker will always show the current month.

Q: Can multiple people edit at the same time?
A: Excel: No, one user at a time. Google Sheets: Yes, if you share the file.
