THE LEGAL PROCESS IN 7 STEPS:

1. Formal Warning (14-day remedy period)
2. Quit Notice (1-3 month notice, depending on tenancy term)
3. Rent Control Filing (meet with Rent Officer)
4. Form 9 Application (official application to court)
5. Rent Officer Mediation (final attempt to resolve)
6. Rent Magistrate Court Hearing (judge decides)
7. Recovery of Possession Order (you get your property back, or enforcement if needed)
